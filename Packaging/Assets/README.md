# Ziki identity

The split Z is two complementary, resonating ribbons: a small nod to Ziqi
and the idea of a listener who understands the meaning behind the sound.
Evergreen `#172E2A`, warm ivory `#F4ECD8`. No microphone or AI sparkle motif.

Masters were generated with built-in imagegen, then exported to macOS sizes
with AppKit and iconutil. Run `swift scripts/generate-brand-assets.swift`.
The generator only resizes the checked-in masters; it does not redraw a
different logo or require a service/API key.

Icon prompt: A completely opaque evergreen square, centered warm-ivory
rounded Z made of two complementary ribbons separated by a clean diagonal
negative-space slit. Flat, simple, quiet, legible at 16px. No text, mockup,
microphone, sparkle, shadow, or border.

Menu template prompt: Preserve that exact two-ribbon silhouette; solid black
on genuine transparent background, tightly framed with 5% margin. Preserve
the transparent diagonal slit. No redesign or extra elements.
